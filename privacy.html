Privacy policy

Morning Log is a private tool used only by its owner.

It reads the owner's own Google Health data (sleep, activity and health measurements), read-only. The data stays on the owner's device. Brief summaries may be used in private text generation under the owner's own account to write the owner's own reminders. Data is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
